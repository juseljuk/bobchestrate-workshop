================================================================================
PATTERN RECOGNITION GUIDE
================================================================================

WEATHER-DRIVEN SPIKES
---------------------
Characteristics:
  - Sudden velocity increase (> 5x)
  - Seasonal product alignment
  - Regional concentration
  - Short duration (12-48 hours)
Typical Response: Rush reorder, surge pricing, regional transfers

SEASONAL PATTERNS
-----------------
Characteristics:
  - Gradual velocity increase (2-4x)
  - Predictable timing
  - Extended duration (weeks)
  - Category-wide trend
Typical Response: Standard priority reorder, modest pricing adjustment

SOCIAL MEDIA TRENDS
-------------------
Characteristics:
  - Rapid velocity spike (3-6x)
  - Multi-location impact
  - Unpredictable timing
  - Variable duration (days to weeks)
Typical Response: Fast reorder, moderate surge pricing, marketing coordination

POST-STOCKOUT RECOVERY
----------------------
Characteristics:
  - Elevated velocity (3-5x)
  - Follows stockout incident
  - Pent-up demand
  - Gradual return to baseline
Typical Response: Rush reorder, rebuild safety stock, premium pricing

NORMAL FLUCTUATIONS
-------------------
Characteristics:
  - Modest velocity increase (< 3x)
  - Weekend/holiday patterns
  - Adequate stock levels
  - Predictable behavior
Typical Response: Standard reorder, no pricing change, monitor
